Damian Leschik is Professor of Marketing Management at IU International University of Applied Sciences, teaching at the Aachen and Cologne campuses in Germany. His research asks what artificial intelligence can contribute to market research and where its limits lie. A particular focus is **silicon sampling**: whether AI-generated respondents can replace, or usefully complement, real people in surveys and interviews.

Before joining academia, he spent about 15 years in the biotech and pharmaceutical industry, working in marketing, sales, business development and production at companies including PAION, PharmaCell, tesa Labtec and Biogazelle. His Doctor of Business Administration from Charles Sturt University (Australia) received the Joss Group Outstanding Thesis Prize.

He teaches in German and English, including consumer behaviour, market research, marketing controlling, international brand management, data science and Python. He is a board member of the working group “AI & Brand/Marketing” of the Arbeitsgemeinschaft für Marketing (AfM), a German association of marketing professors.
